Auto Repair Shop Management Platform

A web application running an auto repair shop's day-to-day operations end to end.

Role
Full Stack Developer
Category
Full Stack

Overview

A management application covering the operational core of an auto repair shop: customers, their vehicles, the repair jobs booked against those vehicles, the services offered, and the business workflows connecting them.

My Role

I worked from the database schema upward — modelling the customer / vehicle / work-order relationships first, then building the interfaces and API integration on top of them.

Key Features

What the system actually does.

Database schema design
Modelled the customer, vehicle, service and work-order relationships that the rest of the system depends on.
Customer and vehicle management
Records for each customer and the vehicles belonging to them, with full service history.
Repair and work-order workflows
Jobs move through defined states from booking to completion.
Service and job management
A service catalogue that work orders draw from, keeping pricing and scope consistent.
Operational dashboard
A single view of what is currently in the shop and what needs attention.
Backend and API integration
Wired the interface to the backend services behind it.

Tech Stack

What it's built with.

React Next.js TypeScript Tailwind CSS REST APIs PostgreSQL

Challenges & Solutions

The problems that shaped the build, and what I did about them.

Challenge

A vehicle outlives its owner's relationship with the shop and may change hands, so history cannot simply hang off the customer record.

Solution: Modelled vehicles as first-class records with their own service history, linked to customers rather than owned by them, so history survives a change of owner.

Challenge

A repair job's scope changes while the car is on the ramp, so a work order cannot be a fixed document written once at booking.

Solution: Built work orders as state machines with defined transitions, so added services and revised estimates are recorded as changes rather than overwriting the original.

Results

What shipped, and what changed because of it.

  • Shop operations — customers, vehicles, jobs and services — run from one application rather than separate records.
  • Service history stays attached to the vehicle across ownership changes.
