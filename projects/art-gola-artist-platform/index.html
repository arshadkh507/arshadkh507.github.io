Overview

A platform for artists that manages the whole journey rather than one step of it — an artist applies, is reviewed and approved, is onboarded, pays, builds a profile, and is published. Each stage has to hand cleanly to the next without an administrator stitching them together manually.

My Role

I work across the flow end to end, including the application form and its validation, the approval and onboarding path, checkout, profile creation and editing, and the website-import workflow — alongside comprehensive QA and live production testing.

Key Features

What the system actually does.

End-to-end application flow
One continuous path from first application through to publication.
Form validation and testing
Validation on the application form, exercised by deliberate testing rather than assumed.
Approval and onboarding
Reviewers approve applicants, and approval moves them into onboarding automatically.
Checkout and payment
Payment as a stage of the journey rather than a separate disconnected step.
Profile creation and editing
Artists build and maintain their own published profile.
Website-import workflow
Imports an artist's existing site so they are not re-entering work by hand.
Cloudflare Turnstile
Bot protection on the public application form.
QA and issue tracking
Comprehensive QA with tracked issues, including testing against live production.

Challenges & Solutions

The problems that shaped the build, and what I did about them.

Challenge

A public application form is an obvious spam target, but a heavy challenge deters the genuine artists it exists to attract.

Solution: Used Cloudflare Turnstile, which validates without demanding puzzle-solving from a real applicant.

Challenge

Artists arrive with an existing website and will not retype their catalogue into a new form.

Solution: Built a website-import workflow that pulls their existing work across, so onboarding starts from their real material.

Challenge

A journey spanning application, approval, payment and publication has many states, and an artist stuck between two of them is invisible.

Solution: Modelled the journey as explicit stages with defined transitions, so an artist is always in a known state that can be reported on.

Results

What shipped, and what changed because of it.

  • Artists move from application to publication through one continuous flow.
  • Existing artist websites import rather than being re-entered by hand.
  • Currently in active development, with QA running against live production.
