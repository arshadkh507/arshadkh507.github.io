Overview

Hire On The Spot is an AI-powered hiring platform that connects employers with candidates — job listings, applications, interview scheduling and employer subscription plans in one place.

A platform like this earns trust or loses it in its plumbing. An employer who is charged after cancelling, or a candidate who never receives an interview email, does not care which integration dropped the event. My work sits at exactly those seams: payments, automated communications, accessibility, performance and launch stability.

My Role

I contribute to development, integrations and production readiness. That covers the Stripe and WooCommerce subscription workflows, the GoHighLevel integration that drives billing, application, interview and account emails, investigation of the JobFront XML job feed, accessibility and performance improvements, and a structured launch audit — testing the critical workflows end to end, documenting issues, verifying fixes and coordinating them with the client and the integration stakeholders.

Challenges & Solutions

The problems that shaped the work, and what I did about them.

A cancel followed by an instant reactivate

Employers sometimes cancel a subscription and reactivate it moments later. Acting on the cancellation event immediately meant sending a cancellation notice for a subscription that was, by the time it arrived, active again.

Solution: Implemented and tested a five-minute grace window: the cancellation notification waits, and is dropped if the subscription is reactivated inside the window.

A subscription is a lifecycle, not a payment

Plan changes, failed payments, cancellations, refunds, free trials and discount codes each move a subscription to a different state, and every state change has to reach both the billing records and the emails the employer receives.

Solution: Worked through each state across Stripe and WooCommerce, connected the resulting events to GoHighLevel so the matching communication goes out, and tested every path end to end rather than only the successful payment.

Launch readiness across several integrations

Payments, the job feed and GoHighLevel are each owned by a different party, so a launch blocker can sit in a system nobody on the team controls directly.

Solution: Ran a structured launch audit — critical workflows verified, email templates reviewed, issues documented — and coordinated the fixes with the client and integration stakeholders until each was verified.

Results

What shipped, and what changed because of it.

  • Subscription billing covers failures, cancellations, refunds, trials and discount codes, not only the successful payment.
  • Billing, application, interview and account emails are driven automatically from website events through GoHighLevel.
  • Employers who reactivate within five minutes of cancelling no longer receive a cancellation notice.
  • Critical payment, hiring, interview and email workflows verified end to end ahead of launch.
